Key Management

A key pair has two parts: a private key that only you keep, and a public key that you share so others can verify your signatures.

Golden rules

  • The private key is shown and downloaded once. This server never stores it.
  • Never share your private key. Anyone who has it can sign as you.
  • The public key is safe to share. It is stored in the registry below.
  • Generating a key takes a second or two (2048-bit RSA).
Use made-up demo identities in this project, not real personal or banking keys.
