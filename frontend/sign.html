Sign a Document

Choose a document and your private key. You will get a small signature file to send along with the document.

Or paste the key text below.

What happens when you click Sign

  1. The document is hashed with SHA-256.
  2. The hash, signing time and your key fingerprint are bundled together.
  3. The bundle is signed with your private key (RSA-PSS).
  4. You download the .sig.json signature file.
Learning demo note: your private key is sent to this local server and used in memory only; it is never saved or logged. In a real product, signing would happen on your own device so the private key never leaves it.
